How to use
Birthday call: press the phone button, choose what happened, then whether she will visit the shop, and write a note.
Wish on WhatsApp: the Wish button counts every wish you send, with the date and time.
Visit / bill: press the + button on a customer to add today’s bill. It is saved as a visit.
Backup: press Backup data every few days. Your data lives only in this browser.
